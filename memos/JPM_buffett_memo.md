# JPM Buffett-style business memo

## Verdict: Watch the business

JPM reported revenue of 121,649 in 2021 and 182,447 in 2025, while net income was 57,048 in 2025. The ROE check failed because the reported ROE included 12.9%, below the 15% test.

Capital spending was not found, and owner earnings were not found. I therefore cannot judge owner earnings from this evidence: owner earnings positive every year is not known, and owner earnings grew, first year to last is not known. I would watch the business rather than make a stronger business judgment until the missing capital-spending and owner-earnings evidence is available.
